Add the widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Habitscape.
  3. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose a habit.

Questions

The widget says "Choose a habit". Touch and hold it, tap Edit Widget and pick a habit. This also happens if the habit was deleted or archived.

Can I fix a day I forgot? Yes. Open the habit and tap any past day in its grid to mark it done or not done.

Does it sync between devices? Not yet. Your habits stay on this iPhone. They are included in your normal iPhone backups.

How do I lock the app? Open Settings in Habitscape and turn on "Lock with Face ID" under Privacy. Your Home Screen widgets still show your grids.

Does it cost anything? No. Habitscape is free, with no in-app purchases, ads or subscription.

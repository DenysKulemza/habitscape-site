Privacy Policy

Habitscape for iPhone · Last updated 27 October 2026

Short version: Habitscape does not collect any data about you. Your habits stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us or anyone else.

What the app stores

The habits you create (name, icon, color) and the days you mark as done are saved on your iPhone only, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. Your settings (which day your week starts on, and whether the app lock is on) are saved on your iPhone too.

What we collect

Nothing. Habitscape has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use third-party code that collects data.

Face ID

If you turn on the optional app lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Habitscape only learns whether the check succeeded. Your face or fingerprint data never reaches the app.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, your Habitscape data is included in that backup, under Apple's terms and your own settings. We have no access to it.

Sharing

When you use "Share as image", Habitscape creates a picture of that habit's grid and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice.

Deleting your data

Delete a habit in the app to remove it and its history, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

Habitscape collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
